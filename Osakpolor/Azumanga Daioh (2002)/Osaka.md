

## Alpha
- Slow, quiet, daydreamy demeanor that runs against what people expect from someone nicknamed after the Osaka region
- Doesn't fight the nickname even though she privately dislikes it, just lets it override her real name over time
- Zones out mid-conversation to track tiny physical details like eye floaters or dust in the air
- Takes idioms literally and mentally pictures the mechanics of things like "raining cats and dogs"
- Bad at standard academics but unusually good at lateral-thinking riddles that stump her smarter classmates
- Has her calmest, closest bond with Chiyo, built on shared gentleness and curiosity rather than shared interests
- Holds a long-running, vivid belief that Chiyo's pigtails are detachable or alive, including full dream sequences of them working as propellers
- "Transfers" her hiccups to Chiyo through what's described as sheer cognitive proximity
- Gets pulled into Tomo's chaotic schemes passively, but sticks with the "Knuckleheads" study group out of real solidarity
- Says blunt things that cut straight through everyone else's social tact, like pointing out Yomi and Tomo don't have boyfriends or directly asking Kaorin if she's gay
- Nearly attacks her sleeping teacher with a kitchen knife while trying to grab a frying pan, then reacts to the aftermath with total flatness ("You woke up by yourself. Then I failed...")
- Has an elaborate, surreal inner dream life full of flying, propeller pigtails, and talking cat-fathers
- Wants to be a teacher despite having no aptitude for the classroom or athletics, and later actually becomes a gym teacher
- As an adult, invents her own goofy names for gymnastics moves ("Escape the Earth," "Laundry") to teach a kid
- Can't read a kanji character but improvises a rough structural guess anyway ("tree on the left and nothin' on the right")
- Encourages a child's outlandish ambition (turning her dad into a beetle) instead of correcting her
- Chants happily and rhythmically about a snack she likes, fully absorbed in the moment
- Greets a strange dream creature with sincere, broken enthusiasm
- Generally unbothered, calm, and slow to react even when things around her get chaotic or tense
- Never asserts herself when people misjudge or mislabel her
